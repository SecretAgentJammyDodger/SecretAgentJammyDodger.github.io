The semantics and what they are associated with by default:

  • TEXCOORD0 = UV0, which is the UVs that you normally interact with in Maya etc.
  • TEXCOORD1 = UV1, these UVs can be added to allow for more detailed texturing.
  • TEXCOORD2 = UV2, used by the common engines (Unity, Unreal, Godot) for lightmaps.
  • TEXCOORD3 = UV3
  • TEXCOORD4+ = pretty much for extra data and calculations.
