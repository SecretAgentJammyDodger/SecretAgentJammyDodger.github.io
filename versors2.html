Quaternions and Topology part 2

First documented 30th August 2026

So there I was, sat at my computer, doing some more digging and thought into how to make sense
of spaces and topologies, just about everything seemed reasonable... until I came across a word.
A word which seems at first glance, so utterly pointless that I couldn't make sense of it.
Those of you who have gone past vector calculus and have gone to the other side, into the
vast and nearly meaningless and superfluous overly wordy realms of the likes of set theory
have probably felt a similar feeling.
That word which I came across, oh so unfortunately, was 'diffeomorphism'.

Everytime that I read about SU(2) being a double cover to SO(3), I was thinking it was something
a little different to the 3-sphere (S^3), but nope, apparently they are exactly the same thing
just seemingly unnecessarily given different names for whatever reason...
Many such cases in mathematics.

Something like a summary...

  • Versors live in a topological space known as S^3 or a 3-sphere.

So yeah, there's a good chance I have butchered some of the terminology, or am misunderstanding some things,
but the basic gist is too much to write at this time so I will come back to this later...
